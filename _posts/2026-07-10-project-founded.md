Lemonade Stand is now part of [Bussetech Software Studio](https://bussetech.com)
and **playable** at <https://lemonade.bussetech.com>: set your price, watch the
weather, buy a little advertising, and try to turn a profit over a seven-day run.

It's a clean-room homage to the 1973 MECC educational classic — the mechanics are
public game ideas, rebuilt from scratch, with the original honored on the
[history page](/history.html). And each day the stand is worked by a rotating
studio gnome, rendered live from the workforce's own igotchi data.

This is the studio's c-archetype build 1: a faithful small port done in public.
