lemonade is now part of [Bussetech Software Studio](https://bussetech.com):
Lemonade Stand — a modern, mobile web homage to the classic MECC educational business game (1973). Faithful mechanics, clean-room and respectfully attributed; the day's stand is worked by a rotating studio gnome.

This post is the site's first feed item — the studio portal aggregates
`/feed.json` from every project site, so publishing a post here is how the
project surfaces on the portal's "latest across the studio" stream.
